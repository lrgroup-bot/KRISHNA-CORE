# KARMA Protocol + BRAHMA Daily Council

Applies to every KRISHNA agent/shishya, active or inactive.

KARMA states:
- GREEN trusted
- YELLOW probation / independent review
- ORANGE restricted / no mutation
- RED quarantined / no execution
- BLACK retired from active routing; verified knowledge may remain

This is an operational consequence model, not a claim that software feels fear.

BRAHMA chairs a daily council. It inventories active and inactive agents, role,
health, recent work, issues and KARMA state; reviews improvement proposals; and
reports a durable council record to KRISHNA.

Priority order:
1. safety/privacy
2. FREE / ₹0
3. reliability/self-heal
4. efficiency/performance
5. capability
6. UI/UX

Paid execution is never automatic. Normal improvement discovery must prefer
free/local/open-source/free-quota solutions. A paid option is marked PAID_BLOCKED
and may only be presented separately to OWNER for an explicit decision.

Council decisions do not bypass OWNER approval, Shared Action Bus, VISHWAKARMA
engineering isolation, MRITYUNJAY verification, SUDARSHAN promotion or rollback.
